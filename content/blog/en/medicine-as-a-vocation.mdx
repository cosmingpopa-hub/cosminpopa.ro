Medicine asks for more than technical performance. It asks for resilience, clarity, and a reason to continue when training gets hard.

After years in the classroom, I have seen students with different starting points reach excellent outcomes. What matters most is not only talent, but sustained purpose.

A vocation is not about never getting tired. It is about returning, again and again, with commitment.
