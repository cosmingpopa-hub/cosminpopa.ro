For many people, anatomy starts and ends with the dissection room. In reality, modern anatomy extends into imaging, simulation, and data-driven clinical workflows.

CT, MRI, CBCT, 3D visualization, and digital learning tools have reshaped how we teach and apply anatomical knowledge.

Anatomy becomes most powerful when it connects disciplines. That is where education, research, and clinical relevance meet.
