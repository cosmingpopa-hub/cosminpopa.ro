I have been teaching anatomy since 2014. Over time, students have changed: they are more visual, more connected, and more eager to understand why.

Traditional medical education often rewards memorization first. It can pass exams, but it does not always build critical clinical thinking.

That is why I started building digital tools such as NASCORA and the Medical Research Toolkit. Technology does not replace the teacher, it amplifies impact.
